# Hunter discovery volume target

This sprint measures the top of the funnel before pain scoring.

Production target per run:

- collect up to 600 unique tattoo seed posts from the existing bounded Meta hashtag pool
- resolve up to 120 public post authors, in parallel, targeting 100 unique artist seeds
- reuse already-resolved direct-intent artists before spending visual-resolution work
- keep pain evidence hard-gated to 72 hours even when seed discovery uses a 14-day activity window
- report Business Discovery successes and failures separately so zero-media failures cannot hide

No outreach, follow automation, login/session reuse, private access, or anti-bot bypass is added by this sprint.
