# Josh Demand Pilot Bootstrap

Signed-in Empty Chair users can initialize the Josh Daniels @ Blind Wolf zero-data acquisition pilot at:

`/demand-acquisition/bootstrap-josh-pilot`

The action creates or updates the artist acquisition identity and creates the five starter Facebook Marketplace campaigns without duplicating existing campaigns.

The pilot uses:
- Blind Wolf Tattoo, 50 Gaines School Rd Ste 13, Athens, GA 30605
- $100 minimum
- hand/palm-size pricing
- American Traditional and Neo-Traditional
- portfolio-derived subject affinities

Marketplace copy is preflighted through the conservative compliance guard before campaigns are stored.
