# Demand Engine multichannel launch

Demand Engine supports separate tracked campaign rows for Marketplace, Facebook Groups, Facebook Profile, Instagram Post, Instagram Reel, Instagram Story, Reddit, TikTok, Referral / Direct Share, QR / Physical, and custom channels.

The Launch Console at `/demand-acquisition/launch` generates channel-specific copy and preserves each campaign's tracked Concierge URL. It provides copy-to-clipboard, native Web Share where supported, and destination-channel launch buttons. It intentionally does not publish into third-party accounts without an authorized platform connection.

Marketplace retains its separate conservative copy-preflight at `/demand-acquisition/compliance`.
