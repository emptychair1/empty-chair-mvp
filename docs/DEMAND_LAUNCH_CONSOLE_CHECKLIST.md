# Demand Launch Console verification

- Open `/demand-acquisition` and confirm the Launch Console button is visible.
- Create a channel set and confirm five non-duplicate tracked campaigns are created.
- Open `/demand-acquisition/launch` and verify the selected channel has five packages.
- Confirm Copy Full Package copies title, channel-specific copy, CTA, and tracked link.
- On mobile, confirm Share opens the native share sheet when supported.
- Confirm destination buttons open the expected third-party channel without attempting to publish automatically.
- Follow a tracked URL and verify the campaign visit count increments.
